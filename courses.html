Our approach

Education is what the charity is for

Trauma Care was founded in 1996 to address the need for commonly agreed standards of the best clinical practice in the management of trauma. We work towards a universally accepted standard of practice for trauma patients, and we are dedicated to advancing and providing access to essential trauma education at the lowest possible cost.

  • Every session is practical or quiz-based — no lecture-only days
  • The patient journey is the syllabus, from the scene of injury to rehabilitation
  • Multidisciplinary by design: no other organisation brings together such a wide range of professionals
  • Members receive CPD certificates and large discounts on ticket prices

Two days, practical

Foundations in Trauma Course

Postponed for a revamp

The 2025 course was postponed so it can undergo a major revamp. Keep an eye out for the return of the FIT Course in 2026.

The Foundations in Trauma Course is a two-day practical event that follows trauma patients through each stage of their journey, starting at the scene of a major incident and ending with rehabilitation. It is perfect for medical students and recent graduates.

Every aspect of the course is practical or quiz-based.

Topics

  • Scene safety
  • Assessment & triage
  • Trauma resuscitation in ED
  • Trauma imaging
  • Critical care
  • Trauma surgery by specialty
  • Ballistics, burns, paediatrics & silver trauma
  • Hyperacute & ongoing rehabilitation

Studying?

Your university may already be a partner

We have forged partnerships with multiple academic institutions to make trauma education more readily available to undergraduate students. Where your institution is a partner, memberships are discounted to £10 per student and your tutors join at no charge.

While the course is away

Other ways to learn with us

The Annual Conference

Four days at Yarnfield Park, with practical and immersive sessions alongside the lecture streams — pre-hospital emergency medicine, HEMS, paramedic practice, critical care, nursing, military and austere medicine, paediatrics, older people and trauma.

CPD webinars

Live sessions you can join from home, with a CPD certificate for members sent automatically within 24 hours. Over 200 more are in the member’s portal, and the latest poster lists what is coming up.

Books and manuals

We write and publish our own books and manuals. Academic partners receive copies for their library; everyone else can buy them in the shop.
