The conference

Four days of trauma care

Taking place at Yarnfield Park Training & Conference Centre, the Trauma Care Conference brings together experts and clinicians working across the entire trauma pathway — from the point of injury and pre-hospital care through emergency medicine, critical care, definitive treatment and rehabilitation.

Across four days, delegates can expect a varied programme of expert speakers, clinical updates, case-based discussions, practical learning and specialist streams, with time built into the programme between sessions to network, visit the exhibition and share experiences with colleagues from across the trauma community.

Multidisciplinary by design

Something for every part of trauma care

Trauma Care is proudly multidisciplinary. Our 2027 programme will feature a wide range of specialist content, including:

  • Pre-hospital emergency medicine
  • HEMS
  • Paramedic practice
  • Critical care
  • Nursing
  • Military & austere medicine
  • Paediatrics
  • Older people & trauma
  • Allied health professionals
  • Community first responders
  • Major trauma
  • Practical and immersive sessions

Whether you are working on the roadside, in an emergency department, operating theatre, critical care unit, rehabilitation service or elsewhere within the trauma pathway, you will find sessions relevant to your practice.

Why attend Trauma Care 2027?

More than sitting in a lecture theatre

Trauma Care Conference is an opportunity to hear from clinicians and experts working at the forefront of trauma care, explore current evidence and evolving practice, discuss challenging cases and learn alongside professionals from different disciplines.

We have deliberately built time between sessions into the conference programme, giving you the opportunity to meet other delegates, make new professional connections, catch up with colleagues and continue conversations sparked by the sessions.

You will also have time to explore our conference exhibition, meet organisations working across trauma and emergency care, and discover new equipment, technology and innovations.

Most importantly, it is a chance to bring the trauma community together to learn, share experience and improve practice.

Buy now

Flexible tickets

You do not have to attend all four days. Choose one day, multiple days or the full conference, so you can build your conference around your role, interests and availability. Each day has its own programme and specialist focus.

CPD & professional development

The conference supports your continuing professional development. Delegates can reflect on their learning and use relevant sessions as evidence within their CPD portfolio, and certificates of attendance are provided after the conference.

Venue, parking & accommodation

Yarnfield Park brings the conference, exhibition, networking and accommodation together in one location, with free on-site parking for delegates. On-site accommodation is booked directly with Yarnfield Park — please contact them for availability and rates. Accommodation is not included in your ticket.
