FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Here are the answers to some of our most common questions. If the answer to your question is not listed below, please email the office.

How can I access previous CPD webinars?

All of our previous webinars are available to catch up on in the member’s portal. We also publish new webinars on our YouTube channel, but these are only available for a limited time, and we don’t upload all new recordings.

I want to become a member. How do I do this?

Take a look at our membership page, which you can also reach from the Membership tab at the top of this page. You will find information on the benefits of becoming a member of Trauma Care, and how your membership supports the charity in achieving its aims and objectives.

Is there another way to support you?

Yes. We understand not everyone can commit to monthly or annual payments, so we have set up a donation button on our fundraising page, which you will also find under the Support Us menu at the top of this page.

My postal address has changed. How do I update it so I continue to receive my quarterly journal?

Log in to the membership area to update your details. Select Member log in at the top of the page and enter your username and password. Once logged in you will see a Profile icon at the top right of the screen, which takes you to your user details. Update them and save, and your postal address is updated automatically.

I have forgotten my password. How do I reset it?

In a couple of clicks. Select Member log in at the top of the screen and you will get a login prompt with an option for Forgotten password. Enter your email address, submit the request, and you will receive an email with the next steps.
