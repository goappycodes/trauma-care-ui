Donate

Donate to Trauma Care

Every donation funds education for the people who treat trauma patients. We know you might not always be able to commit to a regular payment — any donation is gratefully received, and each one contributes directly to our mission of providing quality education.

Donate now (opens in a new tab) Other ways to give

Three ways to give

Give once, monthly, or add Gift Aid

Choose whichever suits you — every option takes you to the same secure checkout.

A one-off gift

Any donation is gratefully received. Give what you can, whenever you can — there is no minimum, and every gift contributes directly to our mission of providing quality education.

Give a one-off gift (opens in a new tab)

A monthly gift

Give once or monthly — a regular gift, of any size, gives Trauma Care a predictable income to plan its education programme around, rather than reacting one appeal at a time.

Set up a monthly gift (opens in a new tab)

Add Gift Aid

If you are a UK taxpayer, add Gift Aid and we receive 25% more, at no extra cost to you — the standard HMRC uplift on every pound you give.

Give with Gift Aid (opens in a new tab)

Donations are handled by PayPal Giving Fund. You will choose the amount, frequency and Gift Aid on their secure site.

Why it matters

Your gift keeps education affordable

We are dedicated to advancing and providing access to essential trauma education at the lowest possible cost. That only works if it is supported — by sponsors, by members, and by people who simply want to help.

  • Every year we deliver high-quality, affordable learning events across the whole chain of trauma care
  • Members receive CPD certificates and large discounts on ticket prices, which your donation helps make possible for everyone else
  • We have forged partnerships with multiple academic institutions to make trauma education more readily available to undergraduate students
  • No other organisation brings together such a wide range of professionals — from doctors and paramedics to military medical personnel and administrators
